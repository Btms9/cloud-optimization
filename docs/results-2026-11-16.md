# Results of the GCP session of 16 November 2026

Run from WSL (Ubuntu) with `docs/gcp-session-procedure.md`, with Youssef's
explicit approval to create and to delete the VMs. 3 VMs (`e2-standard-2`,
`e2-highcpu-4`, `e2-highmem-2`, europe-west1-b) existed from 20:52 to 21:13 UTC
(about 21 minutes, about 0.10 USD). Afterwards `cleanup_gcp.sh` printed
"nothing billable left". 13 runs, all tasks OK (`results/runs/20261116-*`).

## VMs

All three had `Intel(R) Xeon(R) CPU @ 2.20GHz`, family 6, model 79 (Broadwell),
the CPU of the model; no VM had to be recreated
(`data/machine_20261116_<vm>.txt`). The control task `cpu_task 8000000`, alone
on `cloudopt-vm1`, took 7.02 to 7.09 s (6.95 s in the profile; accepted range
6.26 to 7.65 s).

- **Threshold of 15 % per VM (fixed before the session): met.** The 36 per-VM
  errors go from -3.0 % to +12.5 %.
- **The estimator is pessimistic:** 27 of the 36 errors are positive. The worst
  is the overloaded VM of Round-Robin with 27 tasks (164.0 s predicted for
  about 146 s).
- **Consequence for the comparison:** Round-Robin is over-estimated more than
  greedy, so the predicted gain is too optimistic by 2 to 7 points on the three
  task sets.
- **Start delay between VMs.** The measured makespan also contains the delay
  between the first task started on one VM and on another, which the
  prediction ignores: under 0.6 s with 9 tasks, 1.7 s with the 18 tasks
  (Round-Robin), 2.3 to 4.5 s with 27 tasks. This is why the measured
  greedy makespan with 27 tasks (78.7 s) is above its slowest VM (74.8 s).
  The procedure said "under 0.6 s"; that was only true with 9 tasks, and is
  corrected.

## Limits

- One session, two runs per allocation: a measure, not statistics. The 18
  generated tasks are one random lot.
- The 100-seed comparison of `scripts/compare_seeds.py` is still only
  predictions, made with the estimator that greedy optimises; this session
  measured three task sets, not 100.
- The vCPU efficiency (0.63) was not fitted again with these runs.
- The model is valid on Intel Broadwell only; it held on these three VMs.
- The CPU of the old e2-standard-2 measurements (cpu, memory, matrix) stays
  `declared`: the control task, 1.5 % off the profile on a Broadwell VM, makes
  it likely, not proven. `sort_task` on e2-standard-2 is now measured, on two
  sizes only.
- The generated lot has memory tasks of 1.3 to 1.6 s: its makespan depends
  almost only on `cpu_task` and `matrix_task`.
- Cost was not measured as an objective.
- Once `sort_task` is measured, `gen_tasks.py` and `compare_seeds.py` draw it by
  default and give other lots than before for the same seed;
  `data/tasks_gen_s1.csv` is kept as a file and is not affected.

## Possible improvement (not done)

**Fit the vCPU efficiency again** with these 12 runs (add them to `RUNS` in
`scripts/fit_sharing.py`, same machine files). They hold up to 10 tasks on a
VM, far more than the runs 0.63 was calibrated on, and the 27 of 36 positive
errors above suggest 0.63 may be a little low when many tasks share a VM.
Decided on 16/11 not to do it for now: it would change the greedy allocations,
so the committed allocations, the predictions of this file and the 100-seed
figures would all have to be recomputed.
